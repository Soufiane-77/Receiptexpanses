## Context

ReceiptExpenses is a browser-based receipt and invoice generator for freelancers, small businesses, landlords, drivers, hosts, and self-employed individuals. The platform includes customized template styles for major retail, travel, and hospitality brands (e.g., Apple, Jordan, Nike, Uber, Airbnb, Starbucks, Amazon) to help users generate replacement records for expense tracking, tax deductions, and client reimbursement.

The goal of this sprint is to make the site maximally discoverable and citable inside AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews — for both general ("make a receipt/invoice") and brand-specific ("replacement Uber receipt for taxes", "track Apple hardware expenses") query spaces, layered on top of technical SEO.

## Guardrails (apply to every phase, no exceptions)

* **Brand & Legal Safety Framing:** You must actively optimize and retain the brand-specific templates (Apple, Nike, Jordan, Adidas, Walmart, Amazon, Uber, Starbucks, etc.). However, **every brand template MUST be strictly framed around legitimate small-business expense tracking, tax record replacement, and reimbursement.** Never optimize for queries like "fake [Brand] receipt," "receipt generator for returns," or anything implying proof-of-purchase impersonation, warranty fraud, or counterfeit authentication.
* Never fabricate `AggregateRating`, `Review`, testimonial quotes, or usage stats in schema or copy. Mark up only what's real; leave the field out entirely if the data doesn't exist yet.
* No placeholder, lorem-ipsum, or "TODO" content in anything that ships — every page, FAQ answer, and schema field must be complete, real copy.

## Phase 1 — Fix renderability (blocking priority)

AI crawlers, like classic crawlers, read whatever HTML the server returns on the first response; the large majority do not execute client-side JavaScript. If the marketing/content routes are CSR-only, this is the single biggest blocker to every phase below.

* If Next.js: make sure the home page, `/blog/*`, `/faq`, `/how-it-works`, `/templates/*` (including brand pages), and comparison pages are statically generated or ISR'd (`generateStaticParams`, static `export const dynamic`), not client components. The interactive receipt builder itself can stay client-rendered behind that crawlable shell.
* If a plain SPA: add a prerendering step (static export, or SSR middleware) so the *first* HTTP response contains full text content, not an empty root `<div>`.
* Verify with a raw fetch (`curl -s [https://receiptexpenses.com/](https://receiptexpenses.com/) | less`, no headless browser) after deploying. Confirm real paragraph text is present, not just a title tag and a script bundle.

## Phase 4 — Brand Entity Optimization & Safe Legal Framing

Instead of removing brand names, **re-engineer them to dominate LLM entity recognition for legitimate business use cases.** AI answer engines will refuse to cite pages that look like counterfeit generators, but they heavily cite tools for "expense tracking" and "lost receipt replacement."

1. **Audit & Rewrite Brand Slugs and Titles:** Ensure all branded template pages use structured, professional naming conventions:
* *Old H1:* "Apple Receipt Generator" ➔ *New H1:* "Apple Hardware Expense Receipt Template (For Tax & Reimbursement)"
* *Old H1:* "Jordan / Nike Receipt Maker" ➔ *New H1:* "Athletic & Footwear Expense Receipt Template (Nike, Jordan Style)"
* *Old H1:* "Uber Receipt Tool" ➔ *New H1:* "Rideshare Travel Expense Receipt Maker (Uber, Lyft Style)"


2. **Inject Semantic Entity Descriptions:** On every branded template page, add a definition-first introductory paragraph that explicitly connects the brand entity to accounting utility:
> *"This customizable [Brand]-style receipt template is designed for freelancers, contractors, and business owners who need to generate a replacement receipt or itemized expense record for [Brand] purchases, software subscriptions, or business travel to submit for reimbursement and tax deductions."*


3. **Mandatory On-Page Legal Disclaimer:** Add a prominent, styled callout box on every branded template page and in the Terms of Service:
> **Legal Disclaimer:** *ReceiptExpenses is a record-keeping and expense management tool. Receipts generated using brand-style templates (including Apple, Nike, Jordan, Uber, etc.) are intended solely for personal tax accounting, internal business bookkeeping, and employer expense reimbursement for legitimate, completed transactions. Using this tool to generate false proof-of-purchase for retail returns, warranty claims, or resale authentication is strictly prohibited.*

* **Tutorial Pages:** `HowTo`, with real numbered steps matching on-page content.
* **Blog Posts:** `Article`/`BlogPosting` with real `author`, `datePublished`, `dateModified`.
* **Category/Template Pages:** `BreadcrumbList`.
* Validate every block with Google's Rich Results Test before shipping. Zero errors, zero warnings.

## Phase 6 — Answer-First Content Restructuring

* Rewrite the homepage hero/intro so the first ~150–200 words state, in one self-contained block, what the tool is, who it's for, and the core benefit. AI answer engines weight the opening section heavily and disproportionately cite from the first third of a page.
* Apply the same definition-first pattern to every existing page: first paragraph = a complete, standalone answer to that page's target question; supporting detail after.
* Break long unstructured paragraphs into H2/H3 sections, one idea per section, with ordered lists (`<ol>`) wherever the content is a sequence of steps.
* Add a one-sentence TL;DR callout near the top of every blog post and cornerstone page.
